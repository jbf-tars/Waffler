Email signature

In Mail, Outlook and other email apps, when you start with a greeting or say ten words or more. Where the app allows it, Waffler looks at the text before your cursor, only to check your signature isn't already there. It isn't kept or logged.

Transforms (beta)

Select text in any app, press your shortcut, and Waffler rewrites it where it is. Your app's own Undo takes it back, and the text before and after is kept in your Journal.

Transforms

It is off until you turn it on. When you use a transform, the text you selected is read from the app in front, with Accessibility or by copying it, and sent to the provider you chose in Keys and providers. Nothing is read or sent at any other time.

Your own rules

Prompt Engineer

Turns a rough request into a well-structured prompt for an AI model. It fills in this outline from what you wrote, never invents details, and writes "Not specified" where you said nothing.

The headings stay in this order. Put your own in if you want different ones.

Your transforms

Each one is a name and an instruction. For example, call it Friendly and write "Rewrite this to sound warm and short". Pick it in Run above to use it with the shortcut. You can keep up to 5.

You have 5 transforms, the most you can keep. Delete one to add another.

Command mode (beta)

Hold your shortcut, say what you want done, and let go. Waffler edits the text you selected, writes new text where your cursor is, or opens a web search for a question. Your app's own Undo takes an edit back, and the Journal keeps what you said and the text before and after.

While it pastes

Your clipboard
Unless you keep your text there, Waffler holds what you had copied in memory for about a second, then puts it back. It never stores or logs it.
The text field you paste into
To confirm a paste landed, Waffler reads whether that field changed. It never stores or logs what is in it.
The text you selected
Only when you use a transform: Waffler reads your selection, through Accessibility or by copying it and putting your clipboard back, and sends it to your provider on your own key. It never reads a password field.
The text near your cursor (Command mode)
Only while you hold your Command shortcut with nothing selected: Waffler reads up to 500 characters before your cursor, to give your command some context, and sends them to your provider on your own key. It never reads a password field.
The text before your cursor
Only if you turn on an email signature, in an email app: Waffler reads the text before your cursor to check your signature isn't already there. It never stores or logs it.
